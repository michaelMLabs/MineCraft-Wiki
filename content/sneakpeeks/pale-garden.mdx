The **Pale Garden** is the headline addition in the next snapshot wave. It's a small, eerie biome wedged inside dark forests — and it's the first time Minecraft has shipped an environment with a deliberately *muted* palette.

## The look

Everything in the biome leans gray-green. Pale oak logs, hanging moss the color of bone, and a fog layer that sits about ten blocks off the ground. The lighting model also shifts: torches feel **warmer** here because everything around them is so cold.

<Callout type="info">
  This is the first biome that overrides the global fog distance. Performance impact is negligible thanks to the new fog culling pass.
</Callout>

## New blocks

| Block | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Pale oak log | Bonemeal-pale, no contrast bands |
| Pale oak leaves | Slight transparency |
| Pale moss | Generates on stone and logs |
| Hanging moss | Decorative, breaks like vines |

## The Creaking

The biome's signature mob is the **Creaking** — a tall, statue-still figure that **only moves when you aren't looking at it.** It can't be killed, only outrun. Find and break the **Creaking heart** block hidden nearby to disable it.

<Callout type="warning">
  Don't bring valuables on a first visit. The Creaking won't damage you in the overworld, but blind cliff drops are very real.
</Callout>
