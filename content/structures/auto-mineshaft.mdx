Mineshafts are common underground structures primarily found underground in the Overworld. They consist of interlinked 3×3 tunnels with incomplete rail lines running through them, with oak wood platforms bridging gaps or hanging in larger exposed caverns. Mineshafts are the only structures where cave spider monster spawners can be found. A unique variant of mineshafts made out of dark oak wood can generate on the surface in badlands biomes.
