The **Spring to Life** drop is now live on Java and Bedrock. It's a smaller release on paper, but the polish is felt in nearly every biome you walk through.

## What's new

- **Pig variants** — temperate, cold, and warm, each with a slightly different palette.
- **Cow & chicken variants** — same idea, biome-tuned.
- **Firefly bushes** that quietly emit particles at night.
- **Leaf litter** layered over forest and birch floors.
- **Wildflowers** scattered through plains and meadows.

<Callout type="tip">
  Snapshots in this drop introduced a refined ambient sound system. If you play with headphones, lower your music slider — the new biome bed mixes very nicely on its own.
</Callout>

## Quality-of-life fixes

A short list of changes you'll feel without noticing:

- Boats no longer phase through ice on impact.
- Sleep votes now show in the action bar.
- The F3 debug screen has cleaner column widths.

## What's next

The next drop is internally being called **"Mossy Caves"**, and we have a [sneak peek of the pale garden biome](/?category=sneakpeeks) already in snapshot form.

> Worth noting: this is the first drop to ship simultaneously across all platforms within an 8-hour window. Cross-play parity has never been tighter.
