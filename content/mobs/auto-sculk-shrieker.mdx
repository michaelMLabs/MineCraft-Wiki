A sculk shrieker is a sculk block that will "shriek" if any player touches its top (even indirectly) or activates a nearby sculk sensor. Only players can trigger it. Naturally-generated sculk shriekers also inflict Darkness and may summon a warden if triggered, but shriekers placed by players or sculk catalysts are completely inert and harmless.
