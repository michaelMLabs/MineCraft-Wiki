Emerald ore is a rare mineral block that generates only in mountains and windswept hills biomes. It drops emeralds when mined, or itself if mined with a pickaxe with the Silk Touch enchantment. Deepslate emerald ore is a very rare variant of emerald ore that can generate in deepslate and tuff blobs.
